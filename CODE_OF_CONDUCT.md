# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Shiny AXI to be respectful, welcoming, and safe for people of every background and level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and differing viewpoints.
- Accept correction gracefully and help make technical discussion accessible.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and project-related public spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when behavior threatens the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as practical, avoid conflicts of interest, and respond proportionately. If a maintainer is involved, report the concern to another maintainer or through GitHub's platform reporting tools.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
